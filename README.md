# finance-sales-dashboard-powerbi
This interactive Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of global sales performance and profitability. It transforms raw financial data into actionable insights, allowing stakeholders to track revenue trends, product performance, and regional growth.

Key Objectives:
• Analyze Gross Sales ($127.93M) vs. Net Sales ($118.73M) to understand discount impacts.
• Evaluate profitability across 5 countries and 6 product segments.
• Identify seasonal sales trends and high-performing market segments.

Tech Stack & Skills
• Tool: Power BI Desktop
• Data Source: Financial Sample Dataset (Excel/CSV)
• Data Transformation: Power Query (ETL) for cleaning and attribute formatting.
• Data Modeling: Created relationships between sales, products, and geography.
• DAX (Data Analysis Expressions): Used for calculated measures like Total Profit, Sum of Sales, and Year-over-Year comparisons.

Key Insights
• Top Performer: The Paseo product line dominates sales, significantly outperforming Montana and Carretera.
• Market Share: The Government and Small Business segments account for the largest portion of sales volume.
• Regional Trends: High profitability was observed in the North American and European markets (Canada, France, Germany).
• Seasonality: A significant spike in profit occurred mid-2014, suggesting successful quarterly promotions or seasonal demand.
